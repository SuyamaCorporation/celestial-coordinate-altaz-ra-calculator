Formula Reference

GMST = 6.697375 + 2400.0513368 × T + 0.0000258622 × T² (hours)

LST = GMST + longitude/15 (mod 24 h)

H = LST − RA (hour angle, hours)

Alt/Az → Dec: sin δ = sin φ sin a + cos φ cos a cos A

Alt/Az → H: cos H = (sin a − sin φ sin δ) / (cos φ cos δ)

RA/Dec → Alt: sin a = sin φ sin δ + cos φ cos δ cos H

RA/Dec → Az: cos A = (sin δ − sin φ sin a) / (cos φ cos a)

φ = latitude, a = altitude, A = azimuth, δ = declination
